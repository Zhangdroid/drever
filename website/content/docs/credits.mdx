<section className="credits-reflection" aria-labelledby="credits-reflection-title">
  <span>On time, tools, and care</span>
  <h2 id="credits-reflection-title">A note on how fast this happened.</h2>
  <p>
    Not long ago, a project like this might have taken a small team months—or one determined person
    years—to reach a usable first version. With modern AI, and with decades of open-source code,
    documentation, standards, and hard-won lessons to learn from, a working first version can now
    take shape in days, and a surprisingly substantial one in a week or two. That speed still feels
    a little absurd.
  </p>
  <p>
    AI shortened the distance between an idea and working software. It did not decide why Drever
    should exist. It did not stop on its own to say that a toolbar felt too heavy, a transition had
    no reason, or a slide was trying to say too much. Those judgments—and the patience to revisit
    them—were human. AI made the work dramatically faster. Care is what made the result a project.
  </p>
  <small className="credits-disclosure">
    Yes, this note was drafted with AI too. Keeping that sentence was a human decision.
  </small>
</section>
