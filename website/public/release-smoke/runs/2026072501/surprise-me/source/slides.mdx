Raw weekly volume is not automatically a support rate. If more customers saw or completed the experiment, some increase may reflect exposure rather than added friction. Before the ramp, tag the 12 requests, identify any payment failure, duplicate charge, or customer lockout, and normalize both variants to requests per 1,000 checkout attempts.
</Note>
