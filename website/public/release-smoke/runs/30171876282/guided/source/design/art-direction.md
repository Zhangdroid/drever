# Art direction: Quiet launch control

## Visual premise

A calm launch-control room turns a cloud of possible failures into three bright, owned signals.

## Subject-led system

- **Palette:** near-black navy for operational focus, pale telemetry text, amber for unresolved risk, mint for committed action, and cool blue for neutral structure.
- **Typography:** compact uppercase telemetry labels paired with large, plain-language decisions. System fonts keep the project fully local.
- **Grid:** wide control-room bands, precise tracks, rings, and aligned status cells.
- **Recurring motif:** a low-contrast radar grid. It is prominent only when scanning is the idea and quiet elsewhere.
- **Motion voice:** one sweep to search and settle, left-to-right commit to show process, and a single threshold reveal when three risks are selected.

## Signature moments

1. The opening radar changes the emotional frame from optimism to useful failure evidence.
2. The 20-minute protocol appears as one launch sequence rather than a checklist.
3. A two-pass triage field converts many signals into exactly three owner-ready risks.

## Composition rhythm

- Opening: centered statement plus full-height radar object.
- Protocol: horizontal timeline.
- Generation: asymmetric prompt field and a strict facilitation rule.
- Selection: full-canvas decision matrix with a separate actionability gate.
- Ownership: one large reusable contract, not a wall of cards.
- Close: a sparse 3 / 3 / 1 launch-readiness readout.

## Accessibility and fallback choices

- All essential text is high-contrast and remains visible without motion.
- Amber and mint are always paired with words or structure, never used as the sole signal.
- Motion stops under reduced-motion preferences; Steps retain complete final semantics.
- No external fonts, images, APIs, or licensed assets are used.

## Anti-patterns

- No alarm-red overload, military styling, fake dashboards, or decorative data.
- No fictional product examples that could narrow the method.
- No risk inventory presented as success; the decision is the three owned risks.
- No animation of persistent anchors or simultaneous competing motion.
