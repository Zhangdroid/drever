# Art direction — Pre-mortem launch control

## Visual premise

A calm launch-control room turns an initially empty risk radar into three explicit ownership tracks.

## System

- **Type:** system sans; compact uppercase telemetry labels; large sentence-case claims.
- **Palette:** deep navy canvas, mist-white copy, cyan structure, amber risk contacts, coral escalation, green resolved state.
- **Grid:** generous outer frame, thin telemetry rules, compositions that alternate between radar, prompt, flow, matrix, and ownership ledger.
- **Recurring motif:** concentric radar rings and bearing lines; decoration remains low contrast until it carries evidence.
- **Motion voice:** one bounded radar scan establishes the frame; presenter-owned reveals then lock contacts, selections, and ownership rows into place. No continuous ambient motion.

## Signature moments

1. **Failure is assumed so risks become visible** → empty launch radar → quiet scan with no contacts → three risk contacts appear through a Step → the center resolves to “3 risks → owners” → reduced-motion endpoint shows all contacts and the ownership label.
2. **Discussion becomes a decision** → exposure matrix → unranked candidate field → likelihood × impact isolates the upper-right shortlist → exactly three numbered selections settle → reduced-motion endpoint is the numbered shortlist.
3. **Selected risks become controlled work** → three radar contacts → anonymous signals → each connects to an owner / trigger / first-action row → a launch-control ledger remains → reduced-motion endpoint is the complete three-row ledger.

## Anti-patterns

- No fictional product, fabricated metrics, or decorative dashboards.
- No generic card wall, random gradients, or continuous ambient animation.
- No risk is “owned” without a trigger and first action.
