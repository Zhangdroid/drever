# Presentation brief

## Goal

Enable a small group of product, design, and engineering leads to run a useful pre-mortem and leave with exactly three launch risks that have named owners.

## Audience

Cross-functional product, design, and engineering leads. They understand their launch context, but need a fast facilitation method that prevents generic brainstorming, groupthink, and an unowned risk list.

## Content

- Reframe the launch as already failed so weak signals become discussable.
- Use independent silent writing before group discussion.
- Convert failure stories into testable risk statements with an earliest signal.
- Select three risks through private marking, evidence-based challenge, and forced ranking.
- Turn each selected risk into an owner card with mitigation, signal, trigger, and checkpoint.

## Direction

- Duration: 8 minutes
- Language: English
- Slide density: concise with fuller speaker notes
- Motion intensity: expressive but purposeful
- Visual voice: calm launch control / risk radar; dark technical field, precise rings and tracks, quiet cyan signals, amber warnings, coral only for confirmed priority risks
- Visual references or official sources: none; original local visual system
- Motion or interaction references and tools: Drever Steps and deterministic local CSS only; motion should show divergence, convergence, acquisition, and commitment
- Provided or permitted assets: no external assets; local MDX and CSS only
- Deliverables: live presentation source; no PDF in this protected generation stage

## Assumptions

- The method should be reusable for any product launch rather than tied to one fictional product.
- The actual working session is a focused 25-minute pre-mortem; this presentation teaches the method in eight minutes.
- The group is small enough for each lead to write independently and participate in a forced-ranking discussion.
