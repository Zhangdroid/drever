# Art direction: The decision gate

## Visual premise

An AI workflow is drawn as an industrial rail that moves quickly until a signal-yellow gate marks the exact moment responsibility changes hands.

## Subject-led decisions

- **Signal yellow** marks a human decision because the subject is operational caution, not generalized danger.
- **Near-black ink and pale paper** evoke operating manuals and make the deck legible in rooms and document view.
- **A horizontal rail** recurs because every slide advances one artifact toward an external action.
- **Monospace labels** identify states and controls; large grotesque system type carries claims.
- **Hard edges, registration marks, and numbered stops** make the system feel inspectable and deliberate.

## Signature moments

1. The opening rail stops at a single yellow gate between generation and action.
2. A reversibility/blast-radius field locates the decision boundary spatially.
3. One artifact passes through draft, commit, and broadcast, with two presenter-led reveals.

## Motion voice

Precise state commits. The workflow slide reveals one boundary at a time; persistent rails and titles stay fixed. No ambient loops or decorative movement.

## Accessibility and surfaces

- High-contrast colors and redundant text labels; color never carries meaning alone.
- Diagrams include accessible names.
- System fonts only; no network assets.
- Layouts avoid edge-dependent detail and remain meaningful when Steps are fully expanded.

## Anti-patterns

- Generic gradient backdrops
- Robot, sparkle, or brain iconography
- A review step after every AI operation
- Decorative dashboards or invented metrics
- Motion on persistent slide anchors

## Sources and licenses

No external assets, fonts, brands, data, or source material are used.
