# Art direction — The Night Sky Is a Time Machine

## Premise

The deck behaves like a dark observatory instrument: each slide isolates one luminous piece of evidence while a quiet coordinate grid keeps the audience oriented in time.

## System

- **Type:** large, compact display headings; calm sans-serif body copy; tabular numerals for measurements.
- **Palette:** near-black `#061016`, moon-white `#F3F7F5`, spectral cyan `#72E6E1`, photon amber `#FFC56E`, and muted blue-gray `#86A0AD`.
- **Grid:** wide margins, one dominant claim, one visual instrument, and a small eyebrow that acts like an observatory readout.
- **Motif:** thin orbital rings and calibrated ticks. They carry distance and elapsed time, not decoration.
- **Motion:** quiet state commits through static `Step` reveals. No continuous background motion.

## Signature moments

1. A lookback dial makes the title claim tangible at three familiar distances.
2. A spectrum shifts from emitted to observed wavelengths, turning expansion into visible evidence.
3. A logarithmic cosmic timeline compresses 13.8 billion years into one readable line and isolates the oldest observable light.

## Accessibility and constraints

All visuals use local SVG with accessible titles or adjacent text. Meaning never depends on color alone. Type is sized for a room, labels are sparse, and diagrams have complete static states for document and reduced-motion surfaces. No external assets, fonts, or network dependencies are used.

## Anti-patterns

- Generic star-photo backdrops
- Decorative constellations with no explanatory job
- Dense fact walls or tiny axis labels
- Constant twinkling, parallax, or arbitrary lateral entrances
