# Presentation brief

## Goal

Enable a small group of product, design, and engineering leads to run a useful pre-mortem and choose the three launch risks that deserve named owners.

## Audience

Cross-functional product, design, and engineering leads. They understand the launch but need a fast, neutral way to surface and prioritize risk without turning the meeting into status reporting.

## Content

- Frame a concrete failure without inventing a fictional product.
- Use silent writing to avoid hierarchy and anchoring effects.
- Cluster risks by failure mode rather than organizational function.
- Map risks by evidence and launch impact.
- Choose exactly three with a reusable decision rule.
- Give each selected risk one owner, an early signal, a next action, and a tripwire/date.

## Direction

- Duration: 8 minutes
- Language: English
- Slide density: concise with fuller facilitation notes
- Motion intensity: expressive but purposeful; presenter-driven reveals only where understanding changes
- Visual voice: calm launch control / risk radar; deep navy, phosphor teal, amber alerts, precise coordinates, generous negative space
- Visual references or official sources: none required; original local abstraction
- Motion or interaction references and tools: Drever `Step` reveals; no live or external integrations
- Provided or permitted assets: local MDX and CSS only
- Deliverables: live presentation source; no PDF requested

## Assumptions

- The group can see a shared risk list or add short notes during the exercise.
- A facilitator keeps strict time and records the final three ownership contracts.
- The method must remain product-agnostic and reusable.
