# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Drever to be a welcoming place to learn, build, question decisions, and
contribute. Everyone participating in the project is expected to help create an
environment that is respectful, inclusive, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and assume good intent while discussing the work.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about ideas and code.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and ways of
  communicating.
- Accept correction, take responsibility for mistakes, and help repair harm.
- Keep public discussion focused on improving the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory language, imagery, or jokes.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or publishing private information
  without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make another participant feel unsafe or
  unwelcome.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone officially represents
Drever in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `i@zhuo.dev`. Include relevant context,
links, or screenshots when possible. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept
confidential to the extent practical.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily
restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this code.
Enforcement decisions will consider the context, impact, and pattern of
behavior, with the safety of affected people taking priority.
