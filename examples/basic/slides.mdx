## Motion should carry meaning.

A presentation is a sequence of intentional states—not a pile of pages.

<MotionGroup className="basic-state-path" flow="block" intent="focus">
  <Step at={2} data-testid="step-2">
    <article>
      <code>/2/2</code>
      <strong>Reveal the decision</strong>
      <span>Only when the audience is ready.</span>
    </article>
  </Step>
  <Step at={5} data-testid="step-5">
    <article>
      <code>/2/5</code>
      <strong>Keep the exact moment</strong>
      <span>The URL can reopen it later.</span>
    </article>
  </Step>
</MotionGroup>

<Note>
  Pause at step 2, then jump to step 5. The sparse stops are intentional and should round-trip
  through browser history.
</Note>
