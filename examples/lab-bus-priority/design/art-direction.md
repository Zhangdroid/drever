# Riverton bus-priority pilot — art direction

## Design brief

- **Subject and central claim:** a city council decision about whether to authorize a six-month bus-priority-lane pilot; the supplied evidence supports a controlled test with formal reviews rather than a claim of proven outcomes.
- **Audience and action:** Riverton city councillors should vote to authorize the pilot with reviews at weeks 4, 12, and 24.
- **Source material:** only the fictional evidence in the approved brief and plan. No external research, imagery, marks, or measurements.
- **Dominant content:** route scale, current/expected travel-time metrics, an explicit bus/car comparison, one cost, uncertainty discipline, and a three-gate oversight sequence.
- **Venue and surfaces:** a council chamber or similar projected room, plus audience, speaker, document, export, and web surfaces on the locked 1600 × 900 canvas.
- **Accessibility:** concise English; strong contrast; room-readable labels; complete static endpoints; motion subordinate to meaning and disabled under reduced-motion preferences.
- **Tone and pacing:** civic, direct, accountable, and calm. Fifteen slides in roughly fifteen minutes.
- **Forbidden treatments:** advocacy photography, fabricated maps, transit branding, unsupplied thresholds, network diagrams implying implementation detail, decorative traffic data, or animations that make forecasts look observed.
- **Assets and licenses:** no external assets. All diagrams are original HTML/CSS shapes encoding only supplied values. System fonts require no bundled font asset.

## Visual foundation

- **Canvas:** 1600 × 900, preserved exactly from the approved brief and `drever.config.ts`.
- **Safe area:** 72 px minimum on all sides.
- **Default content inset:** 96 px.
- **Surface ownership:** `design/theme.ts` and `design/theme.css` own tokens, typography, root paint, semantic defaults, and motion cadence. `design/stage-background.tsx` owns the full canvas atmosphere and persistent corridor-line motif. Slide scene CSS owns only foreground scene surfaces and evidence objects.
- **Last-known-good checkpoint:** the approved content-complete Draft 1 established the 96 px content inset, grid tracks, card padding, and readable line wraps. Refinement preserves those foundations.

## Visual premise and system

**Premise:** the council follows one civic route from present delay, through an explicit travel-time trade-off, into three visible review gates and a vote.

- **Primary pattern:** `route-constraint-gate-decision`.
- **Rationale:** the subject is literally a corridor decision; the pattern lets the same route vocabulary explain spatial scope, concentrated delay, expected time change, review gates, and the final motion without inventing a map.
- **Beat mapping:** establish route (slides 1–3) → expose constraint (4–5) → compare intervention effects (6–10) → distinguish forecast from result (11–12) → attach gates (13) → decide (14–15).
- **Recurring focal artifact:** a horizontal corridor line with circular stops, used as live Stage context only when it carries a concrete role.
- **Final static payoff:** the closing motion visibly binds the $6.8M authorization and six-month duration to weeks 4, 12, and 24.
- **Topic fingerprint:** even without titles, the route, intersection, trip-time endpoints, bus/car balance, and scheduled gates read as a bus-corridor decision rather than a generic business deck.

### Type system

- One system sans family: `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`.
- Display: 60–94 px, 760 weight, compact leading, editorial line breaks.
- Numeric: tabular figures, 72–230 px depending on role.
- Body and functional labels: 24–36 px; uppercase labels use 18–22 px only where short, high-contrast, and non-prose.
- Rationale: a familiar civic-document voice with strong projector readability; this is an acknowledged restrained fallback because no municipal brand or licensed face was supplied.

### Color roles

- Canvas `#f4f1e8`: warm public-record paper.
- Elevated surface `#fffdf7`: evidence panels.
- Primary ink `#152522`: near-black green for strong projection contrast.
- Quiet ink `#52615e`: qualifiers and source-state labels.
- Main accent `#0f766e`: bus route, expected bus gain, and recommended action.
- Secondary signal `#d95d43`: delay, trade-off, and active review signal.
- Review highlight `#e3b93f`: governance checkpoints only.
- Dark chapter canvas `#152522` with paper/mint text: opening and closing decision boundaries.

## Signature moments

1. **A controlled test has visible control points** → corridor signal and three review nodes → opening line begins uncommitted → signal draws along the route and settles at the three named weeks → recommendation lands as a bounded decision → reduced motion shows the complete line and all nodes.
2. **The proposed change is an expected eight-minute shortening** → fixed 34-minute and 26-minute trip bars → current route is established → expected route contracts to 26/34 of its length while endpoints and labels stay fixed → the 8-minute difference remains visibly measurable → reduced motion shows both complete bars and labels.
3. **Oversight is part of the proposal, not an appendix** → one route signal and gates at weeks 4, 12, 24 → empty review path → the signal travels through each authored gate in order → final gate remains filled and every label remains readable → reduced motion shows the complete path with all gates settled.
4. **The evidence becomes a vote** → decision route and motion terms → two choices are visible → the pilot side takes the route accent and the closing Stage atmosphere shifts once → the final motion binds cost, duration, and review dates → reduced motion shows the final authorization frame immediately.

## Assumptions and fallback choices

- The corridor is represented as an abstract straight route, not a geographic map; no street geometry was supplied.
- “Bus” and “car” use text labels and semantic color, not fabricated vehicle illustrations.
- System sans typography is a declared fallback; no Riverton brand or licensed font was supplied.
- The warm paper/dark civic palette is an original fallback aesthetic. Its semantic accent roles are subject-led and fixed.
- Native shared-snapshot transitions are intentionally not used: the few concrete carried objects are safer and clearer as a persistent live Stage route with explicit local state. All other edges cut or dissolve according to the Handoff map.
