## The city stayed open.

> “Before this bus, I chose between leaving patients early and paying for a ride home.”

The pilot carried **41 riders per night**. Missed hospital connections fell from **23% to 6%**.

Route 14's added hour became permanent in the autumn timetable.

<Note>
